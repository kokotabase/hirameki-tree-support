FAQ

How do I add an idea?
Tap an idea in the tree to select it, then type in the field at the bottom of the screen and send. A new branch grows under the selected idea.
What is the “Spark Pocket”?
It’s a place to keep ideas that drift a little away from your current theme. Save them with “Pocket” in the input field, then open the pocket with the light-bulb icon at the top right and choose “Connect” or “Branch Out” to add them to the tree.
How do I edit or delete an idea?
Tap the selected idea again to open its details, where you can edit it, add notes, or delete it. Right after deleting, you can also undo.
The tree doesn’t fit on the screen.
Drag to move around and pinch to zoom in or out. The button at the bottom right shows the whole tree.
Where is my data stored? What if I get a new iPhone?
“Trees on This iPhone” are stored only on your iPhone. If you restore a new iPhone from a backup (iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer), your data comes along too. Deleting the app also deletes its data. Room trees are stored on our server, so you can use them on a new iPhone by logging in the same way.
What are Rooms?
Rooms let you grow the same tree together with others. Tap + on the home screen, choose “Create a New Room,” then use the members button at the top right of the room to send an invite link, join code, or QR code. You need to sign in with Apple (or log in with LINE) to use Rooms.
How do I join a room?
Open the invite link, or tap + on the home screen → “Join a Room” and enter the 6-character join code. Rooms never appear in search or public lists.
What can other people see in a room?
Ideas, notes, and display names in the tree are visible to all members. Your Spark Pocket is visible only to you until you connect a spark to a branch.
I don't get notifications, or I want to turn them off.
You're notified right away when other members add ideas, when someone leaves a note on your idea, and when someone joins your room — even while that room is open. Tapping a notification opens the room at that idea. You can also see past notifications from the bell at the top right of the home screen. Turn all room notifications on or off in the app's Settings → “Room Notifications,” or for a single room in the room info screen (people button at the top right) → “Notifications for This Room.” If notifications don't arrive, check that they are allowed in the iPhone Settings app → Notifications → Hirameki Tree.
Someone is posting inappropriate content or bothering me.
You can report or block from an idea's details, by touching and holding a note, or from the room's member list. We review reports within 24 hours and remove content or suspend users as needed. For urgent matters, email info@kokotabase.com.
How do I delete my account?
Go to Settings → “Delete Account” in the app at any time. See the Privacy Policy for details.
