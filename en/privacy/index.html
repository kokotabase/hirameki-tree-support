Privacy Policy

kokotabase (“we”) describes below how user information is handled in the iPhone app “Hirameki Tree” (the “App”).

1. Information We Collect

The App stores the trees, ideas, sparks, and notes you enter only on your device. We never collect or view this information.

2. Data Transmission

The App does not send the information you enter to our servers or to any third party. There is no account registration, and the App does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, location, or contacts.

3. Ads, Analytics, and Tracking

The App does not display ads and does not include any analytics or tracking tools. It does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

4. Data Storage and Deletion

Your data is stored on your device and may be included in your iPhone backups. Deleting the App also deletes its data from your device. You can also delete individual trees and ideas within the App.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as needed and will publish any changes on this page.
