Privacy Policy

kokotabase (“we”) describes below how user information is handled in the iPhone app “Hirameki Tree” (the “App”).

1. Trees on This iPhone

The ideas, sparks, and notes in trees you create without logging in (“Trees on This iPhone”) are stored only on your device. We never collect or view this information.

2. Information We Collect for Rooms

Only when you use “Rooms” to grow a tree together with others, we store the following on our server:

  • Login information: a per-user identifier from Apple or LINE. For Sign in with Apple, we also store a token used to revoke the connection. We do not receive your email address
  • Display name: the name you set (a name received from Apple or LINE is only used as the initial suggestion)
  • Room content: room names and descriptions, ideas, notes, Spark Pocket content, who wrote them, and when they were created or updated
  • The rooms you belong to, users you have blocked, and reports you send
  • When you agreed to the Terms of Use and when you last used the service

We do not collect your full name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or photos.

3. How We Use Information

We use this information only to provide Rooms (login, sharing with members, and real-time updates), to handle reports and prevent abuse, and to respond to inquiries.

4. What Other Users Can See

  • Room ideas, notes, and your display name are shown to members of that room
  • Your Spark Pocket is visible only to you, even in a room. Once you connect a spark to a branch, all members can see it
  • Rooms never appear in search or public lists; only people with the invite link or join code can join

5. Sharing and Service Providers

We do not sell or share your information with third parties except as required by law. We use the following service providers to operate the service (Railway and Resend are based in the United States):

  • Railway (server and database hosting; stores Room information)
  • Resend (to notify us by email when a report is submitted; sends the report content)
  • Apple and LY Corporation (LINE) (for login)

6. Ads, Analytics, and Tracking

The App does not display ads and does not include any analytics or tracking tools. It does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

7. Retention and Deletion

  • Trees on This iPhone are stored on your device and may be included in your iPhone backups. Deleting the App also deletes this data from your device
  • Ideas and notes deleted in a room are kept for 30 days so they can be restored, then permanently deleted from our server
  • Deleting a room deletes all of its ideas and notes
  • You can delete your account at any time in the App's Settings. This deletes your account, login information, display name, Spark Pocket content, and blocks, and revokes the Sign in with Apple connection. Ideas and notes you connected to rooms remain in those rooms without your information (shown as by a “Deleted User”) so other members' trees stay intact. Rooms with no other members are deleted
  • Login sessions unused for 180 days are invalidated
  • Report records are kept to handle reports and prevent recurrence

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as needed and will publish any changes on this page.
